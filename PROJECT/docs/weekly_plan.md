# Five-Week Plan

## Week 1

ADC/DAC terminals and voltage checks, 16 kHz timing, digital and physical
BYPASS, MCU SPI command, Stopwatch/Digital Clock, concurrent baseline.

## Week 2

Known 1 kHz energy detector, fixed-point fixed-frequency notch, BYPASS/FILTER
control and initial suppression measurement.

## Week 3

Sample-drop/FIFO/status design, SPI robustness, fixed-point scaling,
saturation/clipping debugging and longer runs.

## Week 4

Quantitative gain/suppression/latency verification. Only after Stage 1 is
stable: multiple known interference and limited-candidate tracking attempt.

## Week 5

Feature freeze, clean rebuild/re-test, demo rehearsal, PPT/code/evidence
organization. New features are rejected if they threaten the baseline.
